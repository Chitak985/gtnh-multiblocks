GregTech: New Horizons Multiblock Resource Calculator

This calculator allows you to see the resource requirements for multiblocks in just a few clicks, and it also lets you customize the amount of I/O ports a multiblock has and change the amount of resources you would need to build one! You can use the calculator by choosing a multiblock below and pressing "Select".

If you see a multiblock that has a Base Cost of 1x Giga Chad Token, that means it doesn't have a recipe yet.

Things in [] do not get consumed in the recipe. [FLUID] before the name of an ingridient indicates that it is a liquid or a gas and its amount is expressed in liters.
