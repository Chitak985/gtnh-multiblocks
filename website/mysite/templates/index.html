GregTech: New Horizons Multiblock Resource Calculator (2.8.4)

This calculator allows you to see the resource requirements for multiblocks in just a few clicks, and it also lets you customize the amount of I/O ports a multiblock has as well as compare the amount of resources you would need to build one! You can use the calculator by choosing a multiblock below and pressing "Select".

If you see a multiblock that has a Base Cost of 1x Giga Chad Token, that means it doesn't have a recipe yet. If you see Unknown in the machine list, that means the resource breakdown used a recipe that doesn't have a machine assigned to it yet. The screenshot for a multiblock may be missing, you will see a small default icon for it instead.

Make sure to choose recipies you have access to by pressing "Show/hide recipe selections" below. There are often more efficient recipies for things that are not selected by default.

Things that have 0 quantity do not get consumed in the recipe but they are required to complete it. Those items' cost is not broken down and they are normally marked with a yellow "NC" text in-game.

[FLUID] before the name of an ingridient indicates that it is a liquid or a gas and its amount is expressed in liters.

If you change your I/O selections the calculator won't update and you have to select the multiblock again.
